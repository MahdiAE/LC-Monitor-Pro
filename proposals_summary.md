# Redesign Proposals Summary - LC Monitor Pro

This document summarizes the 10 redesign proposals created for LC Monitor Pro, aligned with the authoritative, efficient, and premium design principles of the project.

## KPI Executive Dashboards (5 Suggestions)

1.  **The "Pulse" Dashboard (dash-1)**
    *   **Target Persona:** Compliance Officer.
    *   **Focus:** Overall portfolio health.
    *   **Key Feature:** A central achievement ring showing the current LC percentage against the target, surrounded by high-level financial and operational KPIs.

2.  **The "Risk Monitor" (dash-2)**
    *   **Target Persona:** CFO / Internal Auditor.
    *   **Focus:** Financial exposure and penalty mitigation.
    *   **Key Feature:** A contract exposure heatmap allowing executives to identify high-risk contracts at a glance, paired with a penalty-by-department breakdown.

3.  **The "Procurement Funnel" (dash-3)**
    *   **Target Persona:** Procurement Manager.
    *   **Focus:** The procurement lifecycle pipeline.
    *   **Key Feature:** A modernized funnel view that tracks items from RFP draft to awarded contracts, emphasizing volume and transition states.

4.  **Strategic Achievement (dash-4)**
    *   **Target Persona:** Executive Leadership.
    *   **Focus:** Sector-based performance vs. National Goals.
    *   **Key Feature:** A high-level scorecard showing LC performance across different sectors (IT, Construction, etc.) to inform strategic policy decisions.

5.  **Operational Scorecard (dash-5)**
    *   **Target Persona:** Team Lead / Supervisor.
    *   **Focus:** Daily work volume and officer throughput.
    *   **Key Feature:** Tracks real-time metrics like "Reviewed Today," "New Submissions," and "Average Response Time" to manage team workload.

## Tool/Site Layouts (5 Suggestions)

1.  **The "Command Center" Navigation (site-1)**
    *   **Improvement:** Information Hierarchy.
    *   **Design:** A modern, dark-themed sidebar with logically grouped navigation items (Main, Procurement, Compliance) and improved contrast.

2.  **The "Audit-Ready" Grid (site-2)**
    *   **Improvement:** Expert Density.
    *   **Design:** High-density data tables using JetBrains Mono for figures, semantic status badges, and clear typography to make complex data audit-ready and defensible.

3.  **Bilingual Split-View Editor (site-3)**
    *   **Improvement:** Parity and Accuracy.
    *   **Design:** A side-by-side editing interface for English and Arabic text, ensuring that T&Cs and BoQ descriptions are identical in meaning and professional in both languages.

4.  **The Evidence Timeline (site-4)**
    *   **Improvement:** Traceability.
    *   **Design:** A vertical chronological timeline of all contract activities, audits, and evidence submissions, providing an immediate history of compliance decisions.

5.  **The Settings Hub (site-5)**
    *   **Improvement:** Centralized Control.
    *   **Design:** A unified, tabbed interface for managing organization profiles, user permissions, and system-wide mechanisms, moving away from fragmented settings pages.
